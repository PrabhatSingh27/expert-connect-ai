# Expert Connect AI

## Problem Statement

Users often face issues in their homes or offices such as:

- Fan not working
- AC leaking water
- Refrigerator not cooling
- TV not turning on
- Water pipe leakage
- Door latch broken

Finding the right expert quickly is difficult.

The goal of this platform is to automatically identify the problem, determine its urgency, classify it into a broader category, and assign the most suitable expert.

---

## Roles

### Admin

- Monitor platform
- View analytics
- Manage operators
- Configure platform settings

### Operator

- Verify experts
- Manage users
- Monitor assignments
- Handle disputes
- Manually reassign issues

### Customer

- Report issues
- Upload images, videos, and audio
- Track issue status
- Chat with assigned expert
- Provide feedback

### Expert

- Receive assigned issues
- View issue details
- Update issue status
- Resolve issues
